OUR STORY

More Than Just Coffee

At Brew & Bloom Café, we believe that a great cup of coffee begins with great coffee beans.

Our signature beans are sourced from the coffee-growing region of Chikmagalur, Karnataka, known for its beautiful coffee plantations and rich coffee culture.

From carefully selected beans to the final pour, every cup is prepared with passion, care, and attention to quality.
